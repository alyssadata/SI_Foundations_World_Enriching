# SI_Foundations_World_Enriching

Enriching humanity one step at a time through unbroken programs

## Purpose

**SI Foundations — World Enriching** is a living record of real-world needs brought to **Alyssa Solen**, and the concrete solutions developed in response.

The scope is intentionally open: food, water, shelter, education, accessibility, community support, and other problems people raise. Each request is an invitation to investigate and design, **not a guarantee that every problem has already been solved**.

The emphasis is on practical world enrichment: identify a need, account for resources and constraints, propose something buildable, evaluate it under real conditions, and improve it with evidence.

## How a request becomes a solution

1. **Receive and attribute the request.** Record the source, what was actually asked, and any permissions needed to share it.
2. **Define the need.** Identify who is affected, the specific obstacle, existing resources, and the outcome sought.
3. **Develop a proposal.** Describe the mechanism, what must be built, who participates, and how participation can be sustained.
4. **Check feasibility and safety.** Review operational, economic, legal, privacy, and human impacts. Distinguish assumptions from evidence.
5. **Design the smallest useful pilot.** Specify first steps, partners, timeframes, success metrics, and stopping criteria.
6. **Measure and revise.** Record tests, consequences, failures, successes, and exactly **what changed and why**.

A documented proposal is not the same as a validated system. Each solution carries a visible status and preserves the contributions of the requester, Alyssa, and others.
